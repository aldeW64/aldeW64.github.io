Learned world models in robotics often lack true 3D understanding because they rely on 2D video. This work introduces a 4D Latent World Model that predicts the evolution of 3D scenes in a sparse voxel latent space, conditioned on observations and text. The model can decode to rich 3D formats (e.g., Gaussian Splatting), enabling more complete and consistent scene understanding. Used as a planner with a goal-conditioned inverse dynamics model, it generates higher-quality, physically consistent, multi-view coherent futures and improves manipulation performance, generalization, and real-world deployment.
